MASTER OF COMPUTER APPLICATIONS (MCA)

The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) at Mohandas College of Engineering and Technology (MCET) is a two-year postgraduate programme affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU), Kerala. The programme is designed to develop highly skilled computing professionals capable of addressing the evolving challenges of the global technology ecosystem.

Programme Structure

Duration: 2 Years (4 Semesters)
  • Core Courses
  • Professional Electives
  • Laboratory Courses
  • Mini Project , Major Project
  • Seminar , Viva
  • Expert Workshops

Eligibility Criteria

Candidates who have completed a Bachelor's degree in the relevant discipline from a recognized university and satisfy the eligibility criteria prescribed by APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU) and the Government of Kerala are eligible for admission.

Admissions are conducted strictly in accordance with the regulations of the University and competent statutory authorities.
