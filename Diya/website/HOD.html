HOD - Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

Prof. Sreeja K

HOD, MCA

Prof. Sreeja K. obtained her Master's degree (MCA) from Government Engineering College, Thrissur, Kerala. She joined Mohandas College of Engineering and Technology (MCET) in 2003 as a System Analyst, where she was in charge of monitoring, administering, and analyzing campus automation software, managing the architectural design and administration of the campus network, and integrating data arrays across the institution.

She transitioned to the academic faculty in 2007, serving as an Assistant Professor in the Department of MCA. Since 2014, she has additionally undertaken the leadership responsibilities as the Head of the Department (HOD) of MCA.

Before joining MCET, she worked as a System Analyst at the State Project Director’s Office, DPEP, Trivandrum, leading the state MIS unit of the project implementation body. Her domain expertise included educational planning, budget preparation, and monitoring and analysis of key developmental variables across institutional project landscapes. She was also an active member of the National Implementation Team for DISE (District Information System for Education).
